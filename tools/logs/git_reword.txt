    feat(ui): render hands as card sprites on a felt table
    
    Adds a procedural art generator (art-source/generate_card_art.py) that draws all 52 faces, the card back and the table felt as vector shapes, so the art is reproducible from source at any resolution and carries no third-party licensing.
    
    CardSpriteLibrary maps a Card to its sprite by suit/rank index; HandView lays hands out as pooled, fanned card images and reuses them between rounds instead of destroying GameObjects.
    
    Fixes an information leak: the dealer's hole card was face up for the whole round and the label printed the dealer's full total during the player's turn. The hole card now stays face down until the dealer plays and the label shows only the visible total.
    
    ArtImportSettings forces Assets/Art to import as sprites - this is not a 2D project, so the PNGs would otherwise import as plain textures and the library would silently come up empty.
